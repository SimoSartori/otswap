# Citing otswap

If you use otswap in published work, please cite the paper that describes the
method and its use:

- Sartori, S., et al. 2026, *The Back-in-time Void Finder: dynamical
  identification of cosmic voids through optimal transport reconstruction*,
  [arXiv:2601.15378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15378)

otswap is extracted from the optimal transport reconstruction of
CosmoBolognaLib; please cite it as well:

- Marulli, F., Veropalumbo, A., & Moresco, M. 2016, *CosmoBolognaLib: C++
  libraries for cosmological calculations*, Astronomy and Computing, 14, 35,
  [doi:10.1016/j.ascom.2016.01.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ascom.2016.01.005)

The [method](method.md) page lists the work the method builds on.
